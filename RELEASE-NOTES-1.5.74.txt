Scope

- No playback, content, cache, encryption, pairing, download, OTA, heartbeat,
  reboot, autorun, HDMI-2, or media-pool behavior was changed.

Secondary screens

  Return to Menu, Pause, Resume, Restart, and Exit states.

  geometry for the longest approved description, removing empty side space.
- Reduces the excessive padding introduced in 1.5.73.
- Increases subtitle and duration text while retaining item-copy sizing.
- Prevents divider lines from crossing subtitle text.
- Slightly enlarges white checkmarks without changing blue circles.
- Refines spacing around the BEGIN SESSION play arrow.
- Restores the approved prototype's vertical breathing room between title,
  duration, subtitle/divider, checklist rows, and controls while retaining
  Gabe's wider cards and single-line supporting descriptions.

  confirmation states so wider layouts remain proportionate rather than squat.
- Preserves the established Return-card size while redistributing title,
  duration, divider, checklist, and action spacing so top and bottom padding
  remain balanced instead of leaving an empty lower band.

Safety and verification
- Adds a Bluefin UI gate that checks the 1920x1080 lock, Home bounds, shared
  secondary-state classes, content rail, non-wrapping copy, and Return-state
  vertical rhythm.
